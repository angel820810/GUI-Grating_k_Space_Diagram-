GRATING K-SPACE DIAGRAM FOR RGB FOV
Developed by Yen-Chi Chung (Angel)

I developed this GUI to explore whether RGB light across a specified
field of view can satisfy total internal reflection in an AR waveguide.
It visualizes diffraction orders as FOV squares in normalized k-space
and checks their position relative to the TIR region.

The tool helps compare grating pitch, refractive index, and FOV choices
before examining fabrication tolerance. It uses a square-FOV model and
evaluates directional feasibility rather than diffraction efficiency.

See the accompanying PPT for the model, examples, and user guide.
